# Chosen Approach: Blue Hour Scrapbook

## Design Movement
Contemporary editorial scrapbook meets blue-hour digital diary. The page should feel like a carefully assembled keepsake: expressive, layered, and personal without becoming cluttered.

## Core Principles
1. Specificity over polish-for-polish's-sake: keep the nicknames, jokes, years, and real details visible.
2. Tactile digital material: use paper grain, ruled lines, tape-like labels, doodles, and soft shadows as visual evidence of care.
3. Asymmetric storytelling: let one section read like a letter, another like a timeline, another like a small fan zine.
4. Blue as atmosphere, not decoration: use a range from ink navy to electric cyan, then punctuate it with pearl white and one warm butter note.

## Color Philosophy
Ink navy gives the page the calm of a late-night conversation. Electric cyan carries the spark of game nights and inside jokes. Powder blue softens the experience into something affectionate, while pearl white makes the letter easy to read. A small butter-yellow accent stands in for the warmth of memory without fighting the blue identity.

## Layout Paradigm
A scrolling keepsake with an offset editorial rail. The top uses a split composition with a letter on one side and a “friendship status” card on the other. Later sections alternate between full-bleed blue fields and narrow paper-like reading columns. Avoid a generic centered landing page; use intentional overlaps, side notes, and a timeline that steps across the page.

## Signature Elements
A hand-drawn orbit line around the Andrei monogram; small “24/7” tab labels that reference TWS without reproducing official marks; and a tiny clock-and-star motif inspired by the Nana/Diggie pairing.

## Interaction Philosophy
Interactions should feel like turning pages or touching a sticker. Navigation anchors glide to a section, buttons press in with a paper-like nudge, and the “open the letter” action reveals a folded note without interrupting the reading flow.

## Animation
Use short, soft transitions under 300ms for hover and button states. Let decorative stars drift only when reduced motion is not requested. Entrance reveals should rise slightly with opacity, staggered by small intervals. Never use aggressive parallax or constant movement; the page should feel alive but still hold a quiet memory.

## Typography System
Display: Fraunces, italic moments reserved for emotional lines and pull quotes. Body: DM Sans for warmth and clarity. Labels: Space Mono in uppercase for the scrapbook metadata, dates, and small fan-zine annotations. Use a compact, high-contrast hierarchy: oversized serif headline, readable 18–20px letter copy, and restrained mono captions.

## Brand Essence
A blue-hour friendship keepsake for Andrei, made for the one person who stayed through every argument, laugh, and late-night match. Personality: affectionate, playful, steadfast.

## Brand Voice
Headlines should sound like something a real friend would write after thinking about the memory for a while. CTAs should be invitations, not marketing commands. Microcopy can wink at the shared jokes.

Example lines: “Eight years, one favorite person to argue with.” / “Press play on the memories. The best part is that we always find our way back.”

## Wordmark & Logo
Use a custom “A + orbit” mark: a hand-drawn capital A wrapped by a thin circular line with a four-point star at the upper right. The mark should appear as a graphic symbol without relying on a default text logo.

## Signature Brand Color
Electric Keepsake Blue — #56D7FF. It is bright enough to feel like a screen glow, soft enough to stay affectionate, and ownable when paired with ink navy and powder blue.

## Style Decisions
- No official game or K-pop logos; use original fan-inspired symbols and clearly attributed text references.
- Preserve the user's playful “HAHAHA” energy in moderation so the letter remains recognizably theirs.
- Prefer textured, editorial compositions over uniform cards or generic gradients.

## Style Decisions

- The hero is treated as a scrapbook spread first: the moonlit illustration remains, but it is framed as a taped artifact with a caption strip, paper edge, and handwritten-style margin note.
- The A + orbit and clock-star motifs recur as small ink stamps and sticker seals across the letter, timeline, and closing section.
- Timeline blocks are intentionally staggered and carry different paper tones, tape tabs, and margin notes instead of reading as equal product cards.
- Butter yellow is reserved for memory signals: dates, tape, stars, and the small moments that deserve a visual underline.
